# Rwanda Talent Bridge

MASTER AI APP DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: hiJOBS RWANDA

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a fully functional, modern, premium-quality, 3D hyper-realistic job recruitment application called hiJOBS RWANDA.

The application should connect two main categories of users:

Individuals (Job Seekers): People looking for employment opportunities.

Institutions (Employers/Recruiters): Companies, organizations, NGOs, government institutions, and other employers looking to recruit qualified individuals.

The platform must provide a smart recruitment system that automatically matches job seekers with job opportunities based on qualifications and conditions established by employers.

The application must have an attractive, futuristic, professional, intuitive, and hyper-realistic 3D design.

The platform should be responsive and work perfectly on desktop computers, tablets, and smartphones.

6. INTELLIGENT AUTOMATIC JOB MATCHING AND NOTIFICATION SYSTEM

THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT FEATURES OF THE APPLICATION.

Develop an intelligent, automated recruitment-matching engine.

The system must automatically compare every newly published job with the profiles of registered job seekers.

How the matching system should work:

STEP 1: An institution publishes a job opportunity.

STEP 2: The system automatically retrieves the job requirements established by that institution.

STEP 3: The system compares those requirements against the profiles of registered individuals.

STEP 4: The system identifies individuals who satisfy the specified eligibility conditions.

STEP 5: Every qualifying individual automatically receives a notification in their personal My Messages section.

Example:

An institution publishes the following vacancy:

Position: Accountant.

Education: Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance.

Experience: Minimum 2 years.

Skills: Financial reporting, Excel, bookkeeping.

Language: English.

Location: Kigali.

The system must automatically identify job seekers whose registered profiles satisfy these conditions.

For example, if an individual named Jean has:

A Bachelor's degree in Accounting.

Three years of experience.

Financial reporting skills.

Excel skills.

English language proficiency.

Jean should automatically receive a message in his hiJOBS RWANDA inbox.

Example of the automatic message:

"NEW JOB OPPORTUNITY FOR YOU!

Hello Jean,

An institution has published a new job opportunity that matches your professional qualifications and experience.

Position: Accountant

Location: Kigali

Required experience: 2 years

Your profile meets the eligibility conditions specified by the employer.

You are encouraged to review the job description and submit your application before the deadline.

Click here to view and apply.

Thank you for using hiJOBS RWANDA."

Essential matching requirements:

Notifications must be delivered automatically.

Notifications must appear inside the individual's My Messages section.

The notification should also appear in the user's notification center.

Include an unread message indicator.

Show the institution's name and logo.

Include a direct link to the job description.

Include an "Apply Now" button.

Do not notify individuals who fail mandatory eligibility requirements.

Avoid duplicate notifications for the same user and job.

Allow employers to see the number of matched candidates.

Allow employers to view the profiles of matched candidates, subject to candidate privacy settings and consent.

The system should distinguish between mandatory requirements and preferred qualifications.

A person who satisfies all mandatory conditions but lacks some preferred qualifications may still be considered a match.
